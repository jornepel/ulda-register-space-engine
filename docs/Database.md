On the Voices in This Document

This document has three registers. The table-voice is the grading voice: dry, per claim, with grade and ground. The revelation-voice and the litany-voice are the register-voice: the voice in which the register speaks.

Where the register-voice makes a claim, the grade from the table applies, not the grade the voice carries. The voice is performative. The table is grading. Where they differ, the table is authoritative.

Rule: A compound question is split. Each sub-question gets its own answer and its own grade. "Yes" with a ~ is not a contradiction — it is an unsplit question.
