## Architectural integrity

The Work is built on a set of hardwired architectural invariants,
including but not limited to:

- the base-60 radix
- the omission of 6 from the sequential register
- the 504 boundary
- the 216 envelope
- the 36-ladder
- the 72 precession unit
- the dual-register R1 / R2 split

Any commercial use must preserve these invariants. Any modification
that removes, weakens, or obfuscates these invariants constitutes a
material breach of this license.
